## Full description

Azaan times are everywhere — but the time the jamaat actually starts is different at every masjid. Waqt-e-Jamaat shows the real jamaat times of masjids near you, set by each masjid's own management.

**For namazis**
- Map of nearby masjids with a 1–5 km search radius and the next jamaat at a glance
- Today's azaan and jamaat times, Jumu'ah, Taraweeh and Eid jamaats
- "Next jamaat in 12 min" and walking time, with one-tap directions
- Save your masjids and get a reminder before each jamaat
- Be told when a masjid changes its timings
- No timings yet? See estimated prayer start times and ask the masjid to add its jamaat times
- Works as a guest — no sign-up needed to see timings
- English, اردو and Roman Urdu

**For masjid management (Intezamia)**
- Register your masjid with its exact location
- Update daily timings now or from a future date, and notify namazis
- Schedule Jumu'ah, Taraweeh, Eid and other special prayers
- A reminder the evening before each special prayer to confirm the timings
- See confirmations, timing requests and reports from namazis

Masjids appear on the map after verification.
