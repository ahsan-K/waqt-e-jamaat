# Privacy Policy — Waqt-e-Jamaat

_Last updated: 17 October 2026. Replace the contact details below before publishing._

Waqt-e-Jamaat ("the app") shows jamaat times of masjids near you. This policy explains what
data the app uses and why.

## Location
- With your permission, the app uses your device's location **while you use the app** to show
  masjids near you and walking distance. It is not used in the background.
- Your location is used on your device to query nearby masjids. It is **not stored** on our
  servers or linked to your account. If you deny location, you can still search an area.
- Masjid management accounts submit the masjid's location when registering a masjid; that
  location is shown publicly after verification.

## Accounts
- You can use the app as a guest. A random anonymous identifier is created so you can, for
  example, ask a masjid to add timings.
- If you create an account (Apple, Google or email), we store your name, email address, app
  language and reminder preferences, your favourite masjids, and whether you confirmed a
  masjid's timings.
- Masjid management accounts additionally store the masjid details and timings they enter.

## Reports and requests
When you report a wrong time or request timings, we store your user ID, the prayer, any note
you write and the time. The masjid's management can see reports and requests for their masjid.

## Notifications
Jamaat reminders and timing-change alerts are scheduled **on your device**. We do not send your
data to an advertising or analytics service.

## Services we use
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database) to store the data above.
- Apple Maps / Google Maps to show the map.
- AlAdhan (aladhan.com) to calculate estimated prayer times from a masjid's coordinates
  (rounded to about 1 km); no personal data is sent.

## Deleting your data
You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and data, contact us at
**[contact email]** and we will delete it within 30 days.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children beyond what
is described above.
